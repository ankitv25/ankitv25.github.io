02Fixed income · derivatives · portfolio risk

Convexity Lab

A research and portfolio-decision workstation. It takes a market view, or a real portfolio, from evidence through security analysis, risk decomposition and strategy design to a hedged, stress-tested decision, and keeps the thesis, assumptions and outcome on record.

Six stages

One environment, from research to decision

Fifteen research routes built on twenty-five quantitative modules. Each stage answers a question an investment committee would ask, and each one hands its output to the next. Every figure carries its source and observation date.

Public preview Rates & bonds
  1. 01Research

    What is the market telling me?

    The macro regime as a probability distribution across five pillars, cross-asset discovery across FX, regional equity, duration, credit and commodities, and source-dated capital-market assumptions. Views become ranked equity, rates, credit, FX, commodity and volatility ideas, each with explicit invalidation conditions.

    Macro regimeGlobal discoveryMarket viewsCMAs
  2. 02Analyse

    What is this security worth, and what drives it?

    Cash bonds priced off a bootstrapped Treasury curve: implied yield, z-spread, model versus observed price, a rate × spread P&L grid and a Black–Derman–Toy lattice. Securities decomposed by Fama–French five-factor plus momentum regression; all 11 GICS sectors and 56 industry groups screened before a ticker is chosen.

    Bond fair valueSecurity labIndustries & factors
  3. 03Portfolio

    What do I actually own?

    Upload a broker statement (PDF), a CSV or a JSON file. Every parsed line is reviewed before it reaches the model. Or load any of the five live funds at their exact weights. Returns are aligned in the base currency, FX included.

    Statement importFive-fund handoffBase-currency returns
  4. 04Risk

    Where does the risk really come from?

    Capital weight against risk share by Euler contribution, beta, tracking error, VaR and CVaR, factor and six-asset cross-asset exposures. Today's holdings are replayed through Q4 2018, COVID, the 2022 rates shock, the March 2023 bank stress and the 2024 carry unwind, then shocked forward from the macro regime.

    Portfolio risk labHistorical & forward stress
  5. 05Strategy

    Which structure expresses the view best?

    Ten option structures and a custom multi-leg builder with full Greeks, payoff, spot × volatility P&L and historical replay. A signal lab ranks every structure under one distribution. FX carry uses covered-interest-parity forwards, including INR non-deliverable forwards, and four bond expressions separate valuation, duration, curve and credit carry.

    Strategy studioQuant signalsFX carryBond playbook
  6. 06Protect & decide

    What does protection cost, and what risk remains?

    Hedge alternatives sized toward a target drawdown and compared on cost and pre- and post-stress loss. The decision goes to a research log with its legs and exit scenarios, and the idea can be tracked in a paper incubator with marks, Greeks and P&L attribution.

    Protection analysisResearch logStrategy incubator
Stage 06 · alternatives

Protecting a portfolio

Once a portfolio's risk is decomposed, the Lab compares ways to reshape it side by side. It doesn't stop at one hedge verdict: each alternative is shown with its cost, the loss it removes in stress and the risk it leaves behind.

AlternativeWhat it addresses
Index put-spread overlayNarrows the modelled worst scenario toward a target drawdown, with a short lower strike capping the premium
Protective put, 5% OTM or at the moneyKeeps crash convexity; the at-the-money version protects sooner at a higher premium
Collar on the largest riskKeeps a deliberate concentration while defining its downside
Marginal-risk rebalanceMoves capital out of positions whose risk share exceeds their weight, with no options at all
Sector hedgeReduces the largest sector concentration through a liquid sector proxy, without selling every underlying name
Base-currency FX hedgeSeparates the asset thesis from an unintended currency bet, using forwards or defined-risk options
Liquidity & Treasury bufferBuilds dry powder and reduces forced-selling risk before any derivative complexity is added

Why it was rebuiltThe predecessor framework had a Contraction regime that was mathematically unreachable — 280 months spanning the GFC and COVID without a single trigger. Percentile scoring maps every input to a uniform distribution, and the central limit theorem then collapses the composite toward its centre. That mechanism was published as an appendix rather than patched away; the redesign to expanding z-scores followed from it.

04Allocation implications

From regime to allocation

The dynamic layer. A regime model that never changes a position is a research curiosity — this is where the macro call becomes an exposure change, bounded by rule rather than by judgment.

Five engines · one tilt corridor

DAA — the policy portfolio, run actively

DAA on the dashboard

Five engines re-read at each month end — valuation from a live capital-market assumption, trend, relative strength, the MRS regime, and the size of the diversifier sleeve — held inside a tilt corridor around the SAA, so a tilt can never quietly become a different portfolio. The trend sleeve is sized by the macro state: about 6½% in Expansion and Neutral, about 11% in Slowdown and Contraction, against an 8% policy weight. ValidatedDynamic duration improves both crisis types at once — the rare case where a hedge is not a trade-off. The overlay survives a +2-month signal lag, 15% label noise and 30bp/side costs; acting on the confirmed regime at one-month lag is the honest optimum, since each additional lag month costs roughly 0.02 Sharpe.

Open workMechanical regime rotation is research-only: its frozen-rule walk-forward gate failed, so it is deliberately not published as live. The overlay mechanics above are validated on the prior book; re-validating them on the current taxonomy is open work, not a finished claim.
