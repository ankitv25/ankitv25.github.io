Layer 2 · The bridge

Turning a regime call into an allocation

A regime model that never changes a position is a research curiosity. This is the layer that connects the signal to the book — and the layer where the honest answer is still not yet.

DAA · overlay

Dynamic allocation = ratified static base + MRS overlay

The purpose is to enhance an already-strong base, not to rescue a weak one. Regime-flex mechanisms: a credit toggle driven by the MRS credit pillar, trend scaling as a protection budget, TIPS / commodity / gold reflation flex, and dynamic government duration.

Dynamic government duration — long in contraction, short in reflation — improved both crisis types at once: GFC −34.5% → −33.6% and 2022 −19.7% → −17.5%. The v3.7 overlay added λ=0.6 damping and a growth-Contraction gate to kill whipsaw (−31.1% → −27.5%), and survives a +2-month signal lag, 15% label noise and 30bp/side costs. Acting on the confirmed regime at one-month lag is the honest optimum — each additional lag month costs roughly 0.02 Sharpe.

Gate G1 is currently FAILED on the new six-sleeve base. A frozen-rule walk-forward has not yet passed, so DAA stays research-only and is deliberately not published as live. The mechanics above are validated on the prior book; re-validating them on the current taxonomy is open work, not a finished claim.

Robustness, not a single window

The evidence that separates a durable edge from an end-point artefact.

Endowment SAA

Decision D2 is open, and deliberately so. The Endowment's edge over 60/40 is robust — 91.8% of rolling 10-year windows, 87.7% of bootstrap draws, +0.94pp net of costs. The SAA's realized full-window edge is end-point-sensitive (14.5% of rolling 10y windows); its case rests on forward μ, terminal corpus and crisis shape rather than realized dominance. So the flagship choice is a risk-tolerance statement, not a research question: the Endowment if −45% GFC-depth drawdowns are genuinely holdable at a perpetual horizon, the SAA if they are not. Both books are certified sound. The owner signs.
