Regime frequency, 2003–2026

How the 276 months distribute across the calibrated bands.

The predecessor framework had a Contraction regime that was mathematically unreachable — 280 months spanning the GFC and COVID without a single trigger. Percentile scoring maps every input to a uniform distribution, and the central limit theorem then collapses the composite toward its centre. That mechanism was published as an appendix rather than patched away; the redesign to expanding z-scores followed from it.

03
Allocation implications

From regime to allocation

The dynamic layer. A regime model that never changes a position is a research curiosity — this is where the macro call becomes an exposure change, bounded by rule rather than by judgment.

The SAA base plus a dynamic toolkit

Regime tilts argued from the MRS pillars and held inside a tilt corridor — a per-class band derived from how the regime books actually moved against the static base, so a tilt can never quietly become a different portfolio. The purpose is to enhance an already-strong base, not to rescue a weak one.

Drawdown, before and after the overlay

Each pair is a distinct mechanism tested separately — the walk-forward gate on the whole overlay, dynamic duration on two crisis types, and the v3.7 damping fix for whipsaw.

Static base With MRS overlay

Dynamic duration improves both crisis types at once — the rare case where a hedge is not a trade-off. The overlay survives a +2-month signal lag, 15% label noise and 30bp/side costs; acting on the confirmed regime at one-month lag is the honest optimum, since each additional lag month costs roughly 0.02 Sharpe.

Mechanical regime rotation is research-only: its frozen-rule walk-forward gate failed, so it is deliberately not published as live. The overlay mechanics above are validated on the prior book; re-validating them on the current taxonomy is open work, not a finished claim.
