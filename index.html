About

I came to markets from the loss side.

Five years at EXL building and validating loss-forecasting models across Capital One, Discover and Citi — CCAR, CECL and IFRS-9 submissions that go to regulators and get taken apart line by line — plus investment risk work for a mutual fund client. The question on my desk was never how much we could make. It was what happens to this book under an adverse scenario.

That's an uncommon route into investment management, and it's the reason I build portfolios the way I do: size around what can go wrong, distrust any result I haven't tried to break, and write down what would falsify a call before taking it. An IIT Kanpur engineering foundation means I don't hand off the modeling — I pull the data, write the pipeline, run the validation and ship the thing people read. Now finishing an MS in Finance at Georgetown McDonough and building the research stack I'd want to run capital with.
